# Where Specialty Grocers Are Opening

Query answer, filed 2026-09-22. Synthesizes what the three industry-research
sources say about specialty-grocer expansion geography, in response to the
team's question "What do our sources say about where specialty grocers are
opening?"

## Specialty-grocer-specific geography

Claims tied to a *named specialty operator*:

- **Bristol Farms (13 stores) is opening a 14th location in Hollywood in
  mid-April 2026**, aimed at "theater crowds and tourists streaming down
  Hollywood Boulevard." Its existing footprint is "affluent suburbs from
  Newport Beach to Palm Desert and into Los Angeles neighborhoods." —
  *Hoodline, Sarah Kim, March 31, 2026*
  ([raw](../../raw/hoodline-bristol-farms-2026.md) /
  [analysis](hoodline-bristol-farms-2026.md))

- **Gelson's launched a new "ReCharge" micromarket format** (3,000–5,400
  sq. ft., premium groceries + EV charging). — *ICSC Exchange, John Egan,
  April 24, 2026* ([raw](../../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md) /
  [analysis](icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)). This source does **not** state
  where the rollout is happening — the wiki's earlier "SoCal competitor
  note" on Gelson's (`wiki/concepts/expansion.md`) is an inference from
  Gelson's being a known LA-area chain, not something the ICSC article
  itself says.

## Grocery-sector-wide geography (not confirmed specialty-specific)

- **The Southeast captured 215 of 400 tracked new store openings in 2025,
  with Florida leading**, driven by population migration, business-friendly
  policy, and strong consumer spending. A secondary cluster is the
  **Mid-Atlantic** (NJ, MD, DC/Baltimore corridor) — high-density, affluent
  markets where "operators [are] filling gaps and upgrading existing
  locations rather than pure greenfield growth." — *JLL, Cook / Brecailo /
  Virtue / Mason, February 26, 2026*
  ([raw](../../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md) /
  [analysis](jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md))

  This 400-store figure appears in JLL's report right after its discussion
  of the two winning categories (value operators and fresh-format specialty
  grocers), but the source does not say the 400 tracked openings are
  limited to those categories — treat as general grocery-sector openings,
  not confirmed specialty-format-specific.

- **Aldi is testing flexible, modular store formats in Florida** to adapt
  to varied real estate constraints. — *ICSC Exchange*
  ([raw](../../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)). ICSC itself categorizes
  Aldi as a **discount** grocer, not specialty, in this same article —
  included here for completeness, not as a specialty-format data point.

## Conclusion (not stated by any source — our own synthesis)

Taken together, the only source that ties a named specialty operator to a
specific expansion geography is Hoodline on Bristol Farms — and that
geography is Southern California, not the Southeast/Florida region JLL's
broader openings data points to. Claiming specialty grocers as a category
are concentrating in the Southeast would stretch the evidence; the one
specialty-specific data point we have runs the other direction. If
anything, these three sources suggest specialty-format growth may be more
regionally fragmented than the value-format growth (Aldi) that is driving
the Southeast concentration — but this is our inference, not a claim any
one source makes.
