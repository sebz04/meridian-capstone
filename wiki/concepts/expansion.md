# Expansion

## Known (from brief)

- Pasadena is the leading candidate for the next location
- Leadership wants data to back up the decision before committing
- Past expansion: took over leases from chains leaving underserved neighborhoods
- Growth pattern: 6 to 14 stores in 5 years

## Market context (from ICSC 2026)

**Grocery-anchored investment is surging.** ~$11B in deals in 2025, up 42% from 2024. Blackstone acquired 130 properties for $6.7B. Landlords are actively seeking grocery anchors — Meridian may have negotiating leverage on Pasadena lease terms.

**Specialty format is the growth segment.** Specialty grocers showing ~3% YoY growth vs. plateauing discounters and declining traditional chains. Meridian's format is in the right lane.

**Differentiation drives site performance.** JLL: "The days when any grocery store could anchor a center equally well are fading." Pasadena site selection should account for how well the neighborhood fits Meridian's specific positioning (prepared foods, local sourcing, community identity).

**SoCal competitor note.** Gelson's is expanding with microformat (3,000–5,400 sq. ft.) stores combining premium grocery with EV charging. Understand Gelson's footprint in Pasadena before committing.

## Open questions

- What is the Pasadena hypothesis specifically — demographics, lease opportunity, underserved gap?
- Which existing Meridian store most resembles the proposed Pasadena market, and how did it perform?
- What is the timeline for the board decision?
- What is Gelson's current presence in the Pasadena area?
